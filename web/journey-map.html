Pain points today
No single mobile place that says where to be. The schedule lives on a desktop website.
Travel time, miles and tolls are reconstructed later. There is no reliable record.
When equipment fails, a client has an issue or a lead does not show, the only route is a phone call.
Manual photo transfers are slow and error-prone, and delay delivery to clients.
Slow approval and disputes, because drive time and costs are not recorded reliably.
Assignment time and travel time are paid at different rates, so the total is hard to check.

What the map says

1. The lowest points come after the shoot

Uploading photos and filling in the timesheet are where the day turns into paperwork. This is why the app puts Upload photos first after End Assignment and pre-fills the Timesheet.

2. Trouble needs a fast path

Equipment failures, late leads and client issues are handled by phone today. The app puts the right number one tap away and logs the call, and takes equipment reports in the app.

3. Trust in pay is the thread

Reliable time, mileage and toll records, separate rates and a visible earnings breakdown all aim at one outcome: the photographer can check the number and believe it.

Empathy map: the field photographer

Does

  • Checks the schedule and confirms details
  • Travels and logs start and arrival
  • Shoots, often alone
  • Uploads photos from a memory card
  • Fills in a timesheet with times, miles and tolls
  • Calls the scheduler when something goes wrong

PRD 7, 8.3 to 8.9

Thinks and wants

  • Where do I need to be, and when?
  • Will my drive time and costs be counted?
  • Did my photos upload?
  • Can I be sure my pay is right?
  • Can I handle a problem without a long call chain?
